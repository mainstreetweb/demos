What Yev does

Careful work, measured twice

The jobs where the angles are awkward, the house isn't square, and it has to look right when it's finished.

Stairs & Tread Install

Old houses rarely give you two identical steps. Each tread is measured, cut and fitted on its own.

Finish Carpentry

Built-ins, shelving, framing repairs and the joints that decide whether a room looks finished or nearly finished.

Trim & Millwork

Baseboards, casing, crown and thresholds cut to the wall you actually have, not the wall the plans assumed.

Doors & Hardware

Doors that stick, latches that miss, hinges that sag — planed, shimmed and hung so they close with one hand.

General Home Repair

Drywall, rot, fixtures and the list you've been adding to since spring — handled in one visit where possible.

Repair Before Replace

More than one customer was told a thing needed replacing. Ask Yev first — often it can simply be fixed.
