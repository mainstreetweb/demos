The studio

We don't decorate yards. We compose living spaces — shade where you'll actually sit, paths that invite a slower walk, planting that belongs to this soil and this light.

Founded in Austin in 2010, Verde is a small studio on purpose. Every project is led personally by our principal designers, from the first walk of your property to the last plant set in the ground — and through the seasons that follow.

We work almost entirely with Texas natives and climate-adapted plants, which is why Verde gardens look effortless in August, not just in April.

How it unfolds

A garden in four movements

i

First — the walkWalk & listen

We spend an hour on your land with you. Where the light falls, where the water runs, how you actually live outside — the design starts here, not at a drafting table.

ii

Second — the drawingCompose the design

Concept sketches, then a full master plan: spaces, materials, levels, lighting, and a planting palette chosen for this exact soil and sun.

iii

Third — the buildBuild with care

Our own crews and long-trusted stonemasons build in careful phases — protected trees, clean site, and a designer on the ground at every stage.

iv

Fourth — the yearsTend the seasons

A young garden is a promise. Our maintenance plans keep that promise: seasonal pruning, quiet replanting, and edits as the garden finds its shape.

Good to know

Questions, answered

How does a project begin?

With a phone call and then a walk. We visit your property, listen to how you want to live outside, and follow up with a written design proposal. From there, most gardens move from first sketch to finished planting in one to two seasons.

Will a native garden look wild and messy?

Only if that's what you want. Native doesn't mean untamed — we compose natives into structured, deliberate designs: clipped forms against loose grasses, clean stone edges, clear sight lines. You get the resilience of Texas plants with the polish of a designed garden.

Do you build the gardens you design?

Yes — design and build live under one roof. Our own crews handle planting and softscape, and we partner with stonemasons and carpenters we've trusted for over a decade for terraces, pergolas, and water features. One studio stays accountable for the whole garden.

How much water will my garden need?

Far less than a conventional lawn. Because we plant almost entirely natives and climate-adapted species, most Verde gardens need only establishment watering in year one, then modest drip irrigation after that — many beds eventually thrive on rainfall alone.

What happens after the garden is planted?

That's when the relationship really starts. Our seasonal maintenance plans put the original design team in your garden through the year — pruning with intent, editing as plants mature, and keeping the composition true to the drawing as it grows into itself.
