01 The practice Est. 2009 — Portland, OR

Most plumbing is invisible. You live with ours for decades — so we build it as if it will be seen.

Bluewater was founded in 2009 by Ward Ellison, a second-generation journeyman who kept a simple rule from his father’s shop: the work behind the wall should survive its own warranty by thirty years. We are a small company on purpose — twelve people, four vans, one standard.

Portland’s housing stock is old and opinionated. Galvanized lines from the twenties, cast iron stacks, plaster that deserves respect. That is the work we like best: careful surgery in houses that were built to last.

06 Questions Answered plainly

Asked &
answered.

Q.01Do you handle after-hours emergencies?

Yes. The emergency line is answered around the clock by a Bluewater plumber — not an answering service. Burst pipes, failed water heaters, and active leaks are dispatched first; everything else gets the next open slot with an honest arrival window.

Q.02Are you licensed, bonded, and insured?

Fully. Every person who touches your plumbing is an Oregon-licensed journeyman or a registered apprentice working under one. Work that requires a permit gets a permit, and we schedule the inspection ourselves.

Q.03Which parts of Portland do you serve?

The inner east and west sides — Alberta Arts, Irvington, Laurelhurst, Hawthorne, Sellwood, St. Johns, Mississippi, Montavilla, and the neighborhoods between. Just outside that ring? Call anyway; if we can’t come, we’ll name someone good who can.

Q.04Is any job too small?

No. A washer, a wax ring, a slow drain — small work is how most of our longest clients found us. The van that comes is stocked for it, and the plumber who comes treats it like the repipe we may do for you in ten years.

Q.05How do estimates work?

We walk the job with you, explain what we found in plain language, and put the full scope in writing before a wrench is lifted. You approve the written scope first — no surprises on the invoice, ever.

Q.06Can you work on very old houses?

It’s our specialty. Pre-war Portland homes — galvanized supply, cast iron stacks, plaster walls — are most of what we do. We open the smallest hole that does the job, protect original finishes, and leave surfaces ready for a painter, not a rebuild.
