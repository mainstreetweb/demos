Storm questions, straight answers

From the ground: dented gutters and downspouts, granules piling in splash zones, and soft dark bruises on shingles. From the roof: that's our job. We inspect, photograph every slope, and hand you the evidence — even if the answer is "your roof is fine."

Yes. We document the damage, prepare an adjuster-ready report, and meet your adjuster on the roof so the claim reflects what's actually up there. The decision is always the insurer's — but they'll be deciding with complete information.

Most single-family homes: one day, tear-off to ridge cap. Steep pitches, multiple layers, or complex rooflines can take two. You'll know the plan — and the weather window — before a single shingle comes off.

That's what the 24/7 line is for. We run emergency tarping and dry-in crews during storm season so water stops getting in tonight, and the permanent fix gets planned in daylight.
