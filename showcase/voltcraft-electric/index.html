SEC. 08 /  Specifications — Q&A

Straight answers,
no jargon.

Warning signs: fuses instead of breakers, breakers that trip when two appliances run, warm panel covers, or a 100A service feeding a house with modern loads. We'll run a load calculation and tell you plainly — including when the honest answer is "your panel is fine."

Always, and we schedule the inspector ourselves. Unpermitted electrical work can void insurance and stall a home sale. Every Voltcraft job closes with a passed inspection in your closeout file.

Most installs are a single afternoon once the load calculation confirms your panel can carry the circuit. If it can't, we'll show you the numbers and the options — never a surprise mid-job.

It's worth a professional look soon — both can be safe or hazardous depending on condition and connections. We inspect, document with photos and thermal imaging, and give you a prioritized plan instead of a scare story.
