Why Aircrest

Great comfort is invisible. You never hear the system, you rarely touch the thermostat — the house is simply, precisely right.

Aircrest was founded in Raleigh in 2008 by two mechanical engineers who thought HVAC deserved the care of product design. So we run heat-load calculations instead of rules of thumb, size equipment to your actual house, and tune airflow until every room reads the same number.

The result is a system you stop thinking about: quieter than a refrigerator, balanced across floors, and maintained on a schedule by the same team that installed it.

Good questions

Asked & answered

How fast can you get to us?

Most repair calls are seen same-day or next-day, and we give you a two-hour arrival window we actually keep — if we're running late, you get a call before the window closes, not after. New system installations are typically scheduled within the same week.

Repair or replace — how do you decide?

With math, not pressure. We look at the system's age, its refrigerant type, its repair history, and its running efficiency, then show you both paths side by side. Plenty of our visits end with a small repair and a handshake — a system with good years left should get to live them.

Do heat pumps really work in North Carolina winters?

Yes — modern cold-climate heat pumps heat comfortably well below freezing, and Raleigh winters sit well inside their sweet spot. Sized correctly, one system handles July and January alike, usually more efficiently than the furnace it replaces. Sizing is the whole game, which is why we measure first.

What happens during a maintenance visit?

A 21-point instrumented check: refrigerant pressures, electrical connections, coil condition, drain lines, airflow at the registers, and thermostat calibration. You get a plain-English report with photos afterward — what's healthy, what's wearing, and what can safely wait.

Can you fix rooms that never feel right?

That's our favorite kind of problem. Hot upstairs bedrooms and cold offices are usually airflow and duct issues, not equipment failures. We measure room by room, then rebalance, seal, or zone until the whole house agrees on one temperature.

Which brands do you install and service?

We service every major brand, and we install the handful we'd put in our own homes — chosen for quiet operation, parts availability, and how they perform in North Carolina humidity. We'll always explain why a specific model fits your house, not just any house.
