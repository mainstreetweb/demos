The studio

We clean the way a fine hotel turns a room — quietly, precisely, and with a weakness for perfectly folded towels.

Founded in 2014, Linen & Pine is a small studio on purpose. You'll see the same two faces at your door each visit — people who know that the fig tree's leaves collect dust, that the guest bath gets forgotten, and exactly how you like your throw pillows.

Everything we bring is plant-based and home-safe: cedar and eucalyptus in the bottles, never bleach fumes in the hallway. The scent when you walk in is our signature — most clients say it's the moment they exhale.

Good questions

Before you call

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?
Everything. Plant-based, home-safe products scented with cedar and eucalyptus, HEPA-filter vacuums, and separate cloths for every zone of your home — bathroom cloths never travel to the kitchen. If you prefer a product of your own, we're happy to use it.
Will it be the same team every visit?
Yes — that's the point of us. You're matched with a two-person team who learns your home and keeps its care notes. If one of them is ever away, the other leads the visit so nothing about your routine changes.
What about our pets?
We're a pet-loving studio. Your care notes record every animal's name, hiding spot, and door rules. Our products are pet-safe once dry, and we're careful with gates, crates, and escape artists. Expect your pets to be mildly disappointed when we leave.
How do you get in if I'm not home?
Most clients aren't home — that's normal. We hold keys and codes in a sealed, numbered system with no addresses attached, and we confirm arrival and lock-up by text. Garage codes, lockboxes, and smart locks all work beautifully.
Are you insured?
Fully bonded and insured, and every team member is background-checked and employed by us directly — no contractors, no strangers. If we ever damage something, we tell you first and make it right.
What if I need to reschedule?
Life happens — just call or text by noon the day before and we'll move your visit, no fuss and no fees. We'll always offer the next opening with your own team.
