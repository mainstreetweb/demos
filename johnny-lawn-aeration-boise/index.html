What we do

Healthy lawns start below the surface

Aeration is our specialty — it's in the name. But we keep Boise lawns looking sharp all season long.

Core Aeration

We pull thousands of small soil plugs so water, air and nutrients reach the roots — the single best thing you can do for a thicker, greener lawn.

Mowing & Trimming

Clean, even cuts with crisp trimmed edges. We treat your yard like it's our own — corners and side yards included.

Fertilization & Overseeding

The right feed at the right time, plus fresh seed after aeration to fill thin spots — timed for Boise's seasons.

Seasonal Cleanup

Spring and fall cleanups that get leaves, thatch and debris off your grass so it can grow — not suffocate.
